# Who is in Control? v2: final content review (for approval)

Status: **awaiting your approval. Not published.** All 60 questions with their scores are in `docs/EGO_BANK_REVIEW.md`; the scoring validation is in `docs/EGO_BANK_SIMULATION.md`.

## What changed in this review
- **Scores now describe behaviour, not an even spread.** The previous draft forced every question into 0/1/2/3. Now:
  - 59 of 60 questions use ties (only `sa3` keeps a 0 · 1 · 2 · 3 spread)
  - 13 have no ego-driven option (their worst reaction is self-protective)
  - every question has at least one 0-point reaction
- **Healthy assertiveness scores 0, the same as openness.** Stating your view, asking for fair credit, setting a boundary, staying neutral, escalating openly and naming a feeling are not ego. "1" is reserved for *mild self-focus*, such as explaining before acknowledging or stepping back a little.
- **Only behaviour is scored.** One answer that described a belief ("Assume you're being sidelined") was replaced by an observable action.

| Points | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Open **or** healthy assertive (equal, not ranked) |
| 1 | Mild self-focus: not ego |
| 2 | Self-protective: justifies, deflects, keeps score, withdraws effort |
| 3 | Ego-driven: wins at others' expense, dismisses, retaliates, claims credit, overrides covertly |

**Notable decisions beyond the six above**
- **Defending a reasonable position is not ego.**
  - `fb1` "Point out what was better in your version" is **1**.
  - `fb4` "Explain that the topic needs that detail" and `fb6` "Explain what you actually meant" are **1**.
  - `fb5` "Argue each point until it's softened" is **2**, not 3: self-protective, but it doesn't hurt anyone.
- **Escalation is judged by how it's done.**
  - `fb9` asking another manager openly is **2**.
  - `fb3` getting a decision pushed through "without telling them" was reworded and scored **3**, because it is covert.
- **Boundaries and stepping back are mild at most.**
  - `em1` keeping your distance after being snapped at, `cl4` doing your part and staying quiet, and `em4` working around a struggling colleague are each **1**.
- **Mostly-reasonable answers that still lean defensive score 1:** `sa6` pointing to unforeseeable factors (they may be real, but leading with them deflects), and `em3` clarifying it wasn't your team.
- **Three equally reasonable answers:** `rc8`. Being glad, asking your manager to forward the praise, and asking the client to copy you next time all score 0. Only replying-all to claim the work scores **3**.
- **A belief replaced by an action:** `rc10` "Assume you're being sidelined" became "Hold your own meeting on it, without them" (**3**). The bank now scores no thoughts or feelings at all, and a test enforces this.
- **Questions with no ego-driven option (13):** `sa2 sa6 fb1 fb5 fb9 rc2 rc4 cl1 hl1 hl2 em2 em6 em10`. Their worst reaction is self-protective, which is the honest reading.
- **Strength texts** were rewritten so they fit both open and assertive answers. For example, Feedback: "You take criticism without getting defensive, and say it when you see things differently."

**Unchanged from the previous review:** the 60 situations (apart from the wording changes above), giveaway checks, phone length limits, and the four result categories.
